LEVEL 1

PLAYER IS GIVEN A LANTERN
IT IS DARK, NO ELECTRICITY
PLAYER NAVIGATES THE FIRST ROOM, ENTERS A SMALLER ROOM WHERE ELECTRIC CONTROLS ARE SHOWN
PLAYER PRESSES E TO TURN ON ELECTRICITY (PERHAPS A SMALL PUZZLE)
AFTER TURNING ON ELECTRICITY, USER IS ABLE TO NAVIGATE AND OPEN THE DOOR, OPENING UP NEXT LEVEL


LEVEL 2

NABIGATES TO NEXT LEVEL WHICH IS …
A PAGE IS HIDDEN IN THAT ROOM, BY PRESSING 'E' IT DISPLAYS CONTROLS TO TURN ON UV LIGHT
MESSAGES ARE WRITTEN ON WALLS WITH THE UV LIGHT
